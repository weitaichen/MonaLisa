# Mona Misa 美顏 v0.6: 相機逐人美顏 (per-person beauty in the live camera) design

## 摘要（繁體中文）

1. 相機合照裡，偵測到的每一張臉都能點選；滑桿只調整選取的那個人，換去調別人時，先前調好的人在預覽中保持調好的樣子，沒調過的人維持原圖；什麼都沒設定的相機與原圖完全相同。
2. 每個人的設定跟著那個人：移動、交換位置、短暫被擋住都不會跑掉；離開畫面後 4 秒內從原處附近回來仍是同一人，超過就是新的人（原圖）。不做人臉辨識，逐人設定只存在這次開啟相機期間，濾鏡、調節、質感、美白、銳化仍是整張照片共用並照舊記住。
3. 拍照後預覽與照片裡每個人都是調好的樣子；相機有設定美顏時按「編輯」，修圖會替每個人帶入各自的相機效果並可繼續調整；相機沒有任何設定時、以及匯入的照片，仍從原圖開始。
4. 偵測：前鏡頭單人自拍維持原本的偵測方式（不變慢）；合照（後鏡頭一律如此，前鏡頭在畫面出現第二張臉時切換）改用「合照偵測」：把畫面分成幾塊放大來找臉，直立拿手機時後鏡頭找得到約 3 公尺遠、約畫面長邊 3% 的小臉，前鏡頭找得到後排約畫面長邊 5% 的臉，最多 10 張；人多時預覽自動降低臉部更新頻率，讓預覽平均維持每秒 24 格左右以上。若合照偵測在桌機上達不到流暢度或找臉的門檻，就退回單次全畫面偵測（後鏡頭約長邊 9%、前鏡頭約 12% 以上的臉），並在發布說明寫明（§6.6、§16）。**2026-10-22 桌機量測：流暢度門檻沒過，這一版已退回單次全畫面偵測（§10、§16 第 1 項）。同日你決定：相機加「合照模式」按鈕，預設關閉（K22、§3.13）。關閉時照退回的做法，單人自拍維持原本的順暢；打開時前後鏡頭都用合照偵測，遠處、小的臉也能點選，但人多時預覽較不順。開關會記住；切換時每個人的設定都保留。**
5. 「全部套用」會把選取者的設定複製給畫面中所有人並同時調整；畫面中每張臉有淡淡的框可點，選取的人上方有圓點；一次只調一個人，不需要記住誰是誰。

## 1. Intent and why

v0.5 gives the camera **one** look: `liveFrame` draws every shown track with `look.person` (`live.ts:91-102`), and only the beautified track's eased weight is non-zero (`faceTracks.ts:199-203`), so in a group shot one person, or (under 全部套用) everyone identically, is beautified. The user wants to treat a group shot the way the editor treats a group photo since v0.4: pick a person, adjust them, move to the next, and see everyone keep their own result, then take the picture and keep editing the same people. Three things in v0.5 stand in the way:

1. **Only three faces are tracked, and only large ones are found.** `LIVE_DETECT_FACES = MAX_LIVE_FACES = 3` (`limits.ts:177, 186`), and the camera runs BlazeFace short-range (`tracker.ts:404`: the photo bundle only in IMAGE mode), which finds a face from about 12 % of the frame's long edge (§6.1). A group of four, or anyone a few metres away, cannot be tapped.
2. **There is one look.** `AppState.look` (`ui/state.ts:72-73`) is one `CameraLook` (`types.ts:287-290`); the panel edits it through the one-person lens `lensFor(cameraEdit(look), {person: 'live'}, …)` (`Camera.tsx:78-80`, `edit/state.ts:114-116`).
3. **The editor forgets the camera.** Review's 編輯 opens `importEdit()` (`Review.tsx:87-96`), 原圖 for everyone; only the selected face's seed travels (`ui/state.ts:26-27`).

Identity is the hard part, and the user chose the simple kind (U5: 「做第一種」): no recognition, only geometry over time. The design therefore never guesses: when it cannot tell two people apart, the person who might be someone else starts at 原圖. Losing a setting is the safe failure; giving one person another's settings is not allowed (§2.3).

## 2. Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

### 2.1 Goals

- G1: every face in view down to the floor of K17 (back camera 3 %, front-camera group scenes 5 % of the stream frame's long edge), up to 10, is found and then tappable and selectable (U1), on both cameras, with the phone held upright, while 合照模式 is on (K22); with it off (the default), down to option C's floors (back about 9 %, front about 12 %); any smaller face the detection happens to find is tappable too.
- G2: one person is edited at a time; everyone keeps their own result in the preview and the capture (U2, U3).
- G3: untouched people and a fresh camera are 原圖, at no render cost (U4).
- G4: settings follow people through motion, swaps, brief occlusion and short absences; long absences reset (U5).
- G5: 編輯 continues from the camera result per person (U6).
- G6: ordinary group shots work held upright (K17): three people shoulder to shoulder across the frame (about 6–7 % of the long edge), a back-camera group 2–3 m away (about 3.5–5.5 %), the back row of a group selfie (about 6–10 %). The group path (§6.6), with 合照模式 on (K22), finds them; one detection pass over the frame does not (§6.1 readings; option C found 0 of `upright_far10`'s ten 3–4 % faces, §10).

### 2.2 Non-goals

- Face recognition or any appearance model: identity is geometry and time only (U5).
- Persistence of per-person settings beyond the camera session (K4): no person is remembered across ✕, a flip, 60 s without live frames, or an app restart.
- Per-person 濾鏡, 調節, 質感, 美白, 銳化 or 濾鏡強度 (K2): they stay photo-level, as in the editor (`lens.ts:117`, scope `photo` in `engine/params.ts`).
- More than 10 tracked faces; a notice when there are more (the VIDEO graph stops detecting once it tracks `numFaces` faces, so the camera cannot know).
- A guarantee for faces under K17's floor (3 % of the long edge on the back camera, 5 % in front-camera group scenes) with 合照模式 on, and, with it off (the default, K22), for faces under option C's floors (about 9 % on the back camera, 12 % on the front: the solo path keeps v0.5's one pass).
- 美體 in the camera, ↶／↷ and 重置 in the camera (none exist today; §3.10).
- Detection off the main thread (a Worker): not in this step; PLN's fallback (§6.2 F, §10).

### 2.3 Anti-goals (must never happen)

- A1: **the single-face selfie preview gets slower or less smooth than v0.5.** Measured as S §6.3 PL2 (one face in view: detection p50 at the shipped `numFaces` minus p50 at `numFaces` 1 ≤ 5 ms) on the front camera's solo path, plus: with one face, the detection runs every frame (no cadence) and the render input equals v0.5's for the same params. With 合照模式 off (the default, K22) a solo selfie never leaves the solo path, whatever the face count. §6.1: front `numFaces` 10 costs what v0.5's 3 costs (14.1 vs 14.2 ms GPU, 14.1 vs 14.3 ms CPU). With 合照模式 on the user has chosen the group path for every scene on both cameras, a lone selfie included: A1 is the off state's guarantee, not the on state's (a lone face then costs one whole-view IMAGE call per frame, p50 18.3 ms GPU, 22.1 ms CPU on the short-range detector, row L of §6.1's plan measurements; the back camera's lone face 23.3 / 22.6 ms with the render, PLB).
- A2: **one person's settings are applied to another person.** Every ambiguous case resolves to a new person at 原圖 (§7). The residual case the user accepted with U5, someone else entering where a person left within 4 s, is §14 R3.
- A3: **a fresh camera differs from the original.** With no person edited and the photo look neutral, the frame plan is the composite pass only, the same plan a face-free v0.5 frame at 原圖 runs (`pipeline.ts:203-231`), so the preview equals 按住對比's original render of the same frame and the capture is drawn by the composite pass alone (§12.2 E1).

## 3. User-visible behaviour

### 3.1 Faces, taps, marker

- **Brackets** (K12): while ≥ 2 faces are shown and 全部套用 is off, every shown face (found, or held for `LOST_HOLD_MS` after a miss) carries faint corner brackets, the editor's bracket shape at 40 % opacity, drawn by `CameraFaceMarker` (DOM, `pointer-events: none`, never in the capture, `CameraFaceMarker.tsx:1-4`). The selected face has the 6 px accent dot above it (today's dot, `CameraFaceMarker.tsx:100`). While the active panel item is photo-level, brackets and dot dim to 30 % (S A11's rule).
- **Hint:** the first time ≥ 2 faces are shown, a toast 「點選畫面中的臉，可個別調整」 (3.2 s), once per install (key `meiyan.hint.camPersons.v1`, a UI flag, not a setting). With 合照模式 off, the 合照模式 hint follows it (§3.13).
- **Tap** (`cameraTapAction`, `faceTracks.ts:267-275`, unchanged rules): fewer than 2 shown faces → the panel collapse toggles (as today, `camera.spec.ts` keeps passing); 全部套用 on and a face hit → 全部套用 off, that face selected; a face hit that is not the pinned selection (the auto target included) → select it (haptic, the 800 ms accent ring); the pinned face or no face → collapse toggles. Hit-testing grows each box to 44 pt (C1, `faceTracks.ts:239-254`, `coverTapUv` `:278-291`), so a face at the back camera's floor (3 % of the 1920 px long edge, 58 px, about 21 pt on a 393 pt-wide upright preview of the 1080 px width) is tappable. Shoulder to shoulder (centres 2.7 face widths apart, about 57 pt at the floor) the grown boxes do not overlap; in two close rows they can, and the nearest centre wins (`hitTest`, unchanged).
- VoiceOver users still cannot pick a face (S §2.5); they edit the auto target, or everyone through 全部套用.

### 3.2 What the sliders edit

- The **target** is one person: the pinned selection, else the auto target, the largest shown face with S A7's hysteresis (`faceTracks.ts:183-198`). The **first edit** made while the selection is auto pins the auto target (K9), so a face that later grows larger never takes the sliders over silently.
- The panel shows the target's values. Changing the target re-reads every slider and strip; it is no edit.
- A drag keeps the target it started with until it commits, even if the dot moves meanwhile (the person keeps receiving the writes, also while held or away). ### 6.4 Per-frame budget and the degradation rule (K3, K6)

Budget at 30 fps (`camera.ts:22`): T = 33.3 ms per camera frame for detection, render and bookkeeping on the main thread.

- **One face (or none):** the detection runs on every frame (tier L: every second, `liveCore.ts:140-143`, unchanged). This is v0.5's path exactly (A1).
- **Two or more tracked faces:** the loop detects every k-th frame, `k = clamp(⌈(D + R − T) / (T_min − T)⌉, 1, LIVE_MAX_DETECT_EVERY)`, where D is the detection EMA per call (`detectEma`, `live.ts:112`), R the render EMA (`renderEma`), T the camera's own frame interval (starting at 1000 / 30, then the EMA of every frame's step in the frames' own timestamps, rVFC `mediaTime` over the frames presented since the last callback, which no work on the main thread stretches; in the rAF fallback, of the intervals after a frame that ran no detection. As built, round-1 review C16: T was first the EMA of intervals between two frames neither of which detected, which never happens at k ≤ 2, so a 15–20 fps camera in low light kept the 30 fps default and detected every second frame for nothing), `T_min = max(1000 / LIVE_MIN_FPS, 1.25 T)`, `LIVE_MIN_FPS = 24`, `LIVE_MAX_DETECT_EVERY = 6` (face updates ≥ 5 Hz at 30 fps). A k that rises applies after it held 500 ms, one that falls after 1500 ms; `resetSession` sets k = 1. Tier L doubles it: `k_eff = max(k, tier === 'L' ? 2 : 1)`.
  - Rationale: k detections amortize one call over k frames; a call longer than a frame still drops frames, and the rule only bounds how often. Average frame time `(D + R + (k − 1) T) / k ≤ T_min` gives the formula.
  - Desktop, derived from §6.1 (R ≈ 5 ms, the UHD 770's camera frame, adjust spec §6.2): front, 3 faces, D ≈ 27–32 → k = 1 (about 27–30 fps, as v0.5); back, 10 faces, GPU D ≈ 64 → k = 5 (face updates 6 Hz, about 24.7 fps average, one ~69 ms frame per cycle); CPU D ≈ 82 → k = 6 (5 Hz, about 23.7 fps). Bench row LF measures the real numbers (§12.4).
- **On the group path** (§6.6) a detection is one IMAGE call on one view, D is the EMA of those calls, and the same rule picks k (one face or none: every frame, as above; the back camera's lone face is PLB). Each call pays only for the faces in its view, so the cost spreads over frames. Desktop estimate from §6.1 for ten far faces over four tiles, two or three per call: D ≈ 15.6 + 2.5 × 6.5 ≈ 32 ms GPU → k = 1 (about 27 fps), each face refreshed once per cycle of about four calls (about 7 Hz), where C's single 64 ms call needed k = 5 (6 Hz, one ~69 ms frame per cycle). An estimate, measured as PGN (§10).
- **Between detections** the tracks hold their last landmarks (as tier L does today); the smoother is fed only on detecting frames with dt = the time since the track's previous detection (S T4), so any k works.
- **Auto-tier** (`liveCore.ts:8-16, 122-131`): on a detecting frame with k > 1 the work sampled is `R + D / k` (`cadenceSample`); the frames the detection drops are its own, which a lower render tier cannot fix, and they show up on the next frame's callback, which is forgiven exactly those and charged for every other skipped frame, as any frame is (`autoTierSample`). As built (round-1 review C3, round-2 review D3): the detection's frames are those presented in the span it added to its callback, between when the callback would have ended without it and when it did, counted from the detecting frame's own presentation (rVFC `presentationTime`, `FrameInfo.presentationTime`) to the end of its render, `E`: `⌊E / T⌋ − ⌊(E − D) / T⌋` (`framesBlocked`); without a presentation time `⌈D / T⌉`, the most any callback lag gives. As many of them as the next frame dropped are taken out of its sample entirely: out of the frames charged, out of the interval charged for the rest and out of the time the sample is weighted by, since the detection's own sample stands for them. No other frame is forgiven, a detecting frame's own skipped frames included (they came before its detection ran). Round 1 had charged the detection's frames in full on the next frame (a group scene's 1 ms render stepped the tier down to L on both paths, as every LF row of §6.1 ended, C3), then forgave `⌈(D + R) / T⌉` there, a frame more than a callback on its frame's presentation passes, on top of the detecting frame's drops and with the forgiven frame's 1 ms weighted by the whole blocked interval, so one frame of GPU back-pressure at k 2 or 6 never stepped down (about 10 fps at tier H, D3). The count is exact when the callback after the work runs on the first frame presented after it (the unit tests' compositor); on rVFC's own order, the newest frame presented during the work, the next frame drops one frame fewer, and one frame of back-pressure there cannot be told from the detection's and is forgiven: it is charged on the cycle's other frames instead (at k 2 the detecting frame, whose sample the forgiven frame's no longer outweighs). Measured on the e2e Chromium fake camera (2026-10-23, a 112 ms task every 8th callback): `presentationTime` is on `performance.now()`'s clock (callback start − `presentationTime` −0.1 to 19.7 ms, p50 0.1), and the callback after the task ran on the newest frame presented during it (2 of its 3 frames skipped) in 13 of 14, a frame later in 1. A step-down holds per path for the page session (`sessionAutoTier`, `live.ts`): one taken on the solo path holds on the group path too (the same render), one taken on the group path only there, so turning 合照模式 off renders at the solo path's tier again, and a toggle starts a new measurement window (the warm-up included), so the path left behind's frames are never charged to the other; the solo path's is the device's tier that the export and import limits read (`autoTierSession`: tier L blocks 原始解析度 and imports at 1440 px).
- **Capture** (K13) costs one detection when the last one is older than the captured frame (on the group path also when that frame's one call left a face the shot hands over unrefreshed, §6.6): ≤ D on the solo path, at most one cycle of calls on the group path, once per shot. (As built, round-2 review D10: on the group path only the views of the cycle that could refresh a face the shot hands over that no call on the frame fed, never one that answered on the frame. Round-5 review, 2026-10-23: one complete refresh cycle on every shot, the whole view and each tile a track or an away person in grace needs, §6.6 Capture; on §6.1's desktop three near faces take one call as before, and `upright_far10`'s ten far faces three instead of two, about 70 ms more on the GPU and 100 ms more on the CPU at p50, §10.)
- `LiveStats` and the debug state report k (`liveEvery`) and the detector (`liveDetector`).

### 6.6 The group path (K17, K22)

The scope (U1 not narrowed, the floors) is K17; it runs while the user's 合照模式 is on (K22, §3.13). The mechanism below is Claude's call; the plan may change any number in it with a measurement, committed with the numbers in `limits.ts` (S §6.4).

**Views.** In the stream frame's pixels, inside the **visible crop**: the ratio crop the preview shows and the shutter saves (the crop of `shotSeed`, `imaging.ts:79-85`). A face outside it is not in view.

- The **whole view**: the visible crop, copied at most `LIVE_VIEW_MAX_EDGE` = 1280 px on its long edge.
- The **tiles**: squares of side `LIVE_FACE_FLOOR[facing] / DETECTOR_FLOOR[detector]` × the stream frame's long edge (no larger than the crop's short side), in a grid spread evenly over the crop so that neighbours overlap by at least `DETECTOR_FLOOR[detector]` × the crop's long edge (a face too large to sit whole in some tile is one the whole view finds), copied at native size: the detector scales every view to its own input, so a tile needs no upscaling. `LIVE_FACE_FLOOR = { user: 0.05, environment: 0.03 }`, `DETECTOR_FLOOR = { camera: 0.11, photo: 0.08 }` (§6.1's ~12 % and ~9 %, with margin).
- On the 1080 × 1920 upright stream: back camera (full-range, floor 3 %): 720 px tiles, 2 × 3 on 3:4, 2 × 4 on 9:16, 2 × 2 on 1:1. Front camera (short-range, floor 5 %): 873 px tiles, 2 × 2 on 3:4 and 1:1, 2 × 3 on 9:16. Any other stream (a desktop webcam, the e2e's fake camera) uses the same fractions.

**Assignment.** A track is **large** when its box width is at least 1.25 × `DETECTOR_FLOOR` × the crop's long edge, else **small**. A large track is refreshed by the whole view, a small one by the tile that holds its predicted box whole with the nearest centre. (As built, round-2 review D9: holds it whole once the box is clipped to the visible crop, the relation of every scope, §6.6 Mapping and matching, so a small face the crop's own edge cuts is its edge tile's.)

**Schedule.** The group path cycles through its **refresh views**: the whole view, then each tile that holds a small track, in raster order. Every `LIVE_SCAN_EVERY` = 4th call is a **scan** instead: the next tile in raster order that is not a refresh view in this cycle. The whole view is in every cycle, so a new large face is found within one; a new small face within one sweep of the scans (8 tiles at most, so at most 32 calls, about 1.1 s at 30 calls per second). No scans run while 10 faces are tracked. Examples: a group selfie of two or three near faces runs the whole view three calls in four; ten far faces over four tiles run a cycle of five views.

**Mapping and matching.** A call's faces are mapped from the view to stream UV (x and y by the view's offset and scale, z by the view's width over the frame's) and matched as §7.3 against the predicted boxes of every track (§7.1 I2); a pair feeds its track when the view holds the track's predicted box or the face whole, and a pair whose face and predicted box the view's edge both cuts accounts for both without feeding (as built, round-2 review). A face cut by a tile's edge is ignored in that tile unless it matches a track (the overlapping tile holds it whole). Any call's faces refresh every track they match, wherever that track is assigned. A face whose owner turns on a track the call cannot see is left to that track's own view (§7.1 I3). A track is **missed** only by a call whose view holds its predicted box whole and finds no face for it; for every other track the call is no event: it neither misses nor ages it, and the hold (`LOST_HOLD_MS`), the velocity (§7.2) and the smoother (dt since the track's own last refresh, S T4) count from the track's own refreshes. Once a held track's hold has run out, whichever call answers first takes it out of its tracks before anyone is re-identified and weighs its person as the away person their release makes them, then releases it once it has taken everyone it decided (§7.3 item 6, I4), so its person is an away candidate wherever the face comes back (round-2 review D1: round 1 let only a call that can miss the track drop it, and on its own tile's next call a far face back from just over the hold was in neither candidate set, so it got an away neighbour's settings or a new person at 原圖; round-3 review E3: round 2 released it before the decision, and with the away pool full that evicted the pool's oldest person on the very call she came back); and a no-answer (A5) leaves every track found before it **unseen**, eased out but neither held, dropped nor evicted, until a call that can match or miss it answers (`stale` in `faceTracks.ts`; round-1 review C9: one throw while tiles refreshed far faces had another view's call drop them before their own tile came back, after which the tile left the refresh list and the face returned as a new person at 原圖), and every track held before it **held**: known missing, its hold paused (round-3 review E5: marked unseen too, a person who had left was handed to 編輯 as found, §5.3). On the group path a no-answer is one view's: it leaves unseen only the tracks that view could miss (round-4 review F3), and pauses the hold of only those, each track's own pause from the no-answer to the next answer (round-5 review G4: it paused every track's hold, so a view that kept failing, the whole view's 1280-edge copy under iOS canvas memory pressure say, ran the holds and the velocities of the faces it cannot see about a third slow, and a held person went to 編輯 held for half a second longer). At the shutter a view's no-answer is no event at all (§6.6 Capture; round 4's same-frame clause, `detect(null, …, scope, frame)`, which kept a face found on the captured frame from going unseen when a later shutter view failed, went with the per-view shutter calls it patched). New faces start tracks and take persons (§7.3 items 4–5). (As built, the plan's Task 14 fix, recorded 2026-10-22: a view matches the tracks whose predicted box it holds whole **once the box is clipped to the visible crop**, `viewScope` in `liveViews.ts`. For the whole view that is every track partly in view; a tile also matches a box cut by the crop's edge where that edge is its own, and a box cut by a tile's edge inside the crop stays the neighbouring tile's. What a view can miss was unchanged then; round-2 review D9 made the clip the one relation for missing, refreshing and starting too, see below. Without the clip, a face that stopped just inside the crop's edge, or a face cut by the frame's edge whose prediction jittered past it, was matched by no view and started a new person on every whole-view call: `group_move`'s swap, `detector.spec.ts` D2.) (As built, round-1 review C5 and C6, recorded 2026-10-22. **A pair** of a face and a track is in a call's scope when the view holds the track's predicted box **or the face's own box** whole, each clipped to the crop: a face standing on an internal tile edge, whose prediction or jitter puts its track across the edge of the tile that sees it whole, feeds that track instead of starting a second person on the same face, and a neighbour's face goes to the neighbour's track instead of continuing a missed track beside it. Every track weighs in on the continuation decision (§7.3 item 4), the ones out of the call's scope too (people in view elsewhere). (As built, round-2 review D2 and D5, recorded 2026-10-23, replacing round 1's rule that a track out of scope was continued only when held and that a face nearest one found elsewhere fed nobody and started at 原圖. That rule, and filtering pairs by scope before the greedy IoU, let scope decide identity: a neighbour B standing on t0,0's edge took the face of an edited A moving toward B, since B's own cut face was filtered out of B's pairs, or tied A's continuation into a new person at 原圖; and B's own face moving a width into t0,0 got a second person, who then won it. Now the rules pair over every track, B's cut face accounts for B, and a face whose owner turns on a track the call cannot see, B here, is left to B's own view, t1,0, which sees both places, §7.1 I1–I3.) **Starting:** a face starts a track in a tile only when the tile holds it whole (one cut by a tile's edge is the overlapping tile's), and in the whole view whenever it is in the crop, so a face the crop's own edge cuts, which no other view holds whole, is found: a close-up selfie whose forehead or chin passes the preview's edge on 3:4 or 1:1, a head the back camera's crop cuts. (As built, round-2 review D9, recorded 2026-10-23: one relation, the box clipped to the crop held whole (`holdsInCrop`), decides alike which views match a track, which views may start a face, which view refreshes a track (`tileFor`, `ownViewOf`) and which views can miss it, so the view that finds a face keeps it. ## 7. Identity

### 7.1 Principles

Geometry and time only (U5). Every ambiguity resolves to a new person at 原圖 (A2). A person exists as long as their track lives, plus the away grace.

The tracking invariants `faceTracks.ts` keeps, on both paths (recorded in round-2 review, 2026-10-23; D1, D2 and D5 broke them on the group path after round 1, D4 at the cap; round-3 review E1–E3 and E6 broke them after round 2, E3 on the solo path too, and the call is now built so they hold by construction: it makes every decision first, from what it read, then feeds, misses, takes, and only then releases; at the shutter on the group path the answers of one complete refresh cycle are one such call, `detectViews`, round-5 review, §6.6 Capture):

- **I1. One face, one track, one person.** In one call a face goes to at most one track and a track takes at most one face; a person is on at most one track. No call starts a second track on a face a track owns, and no call gives a face to a track that is not its owner (a swap). A track the call decides for a face is that face's whether or not the call can feed it: it is reserved, out of every later face's candidates, neither missed nor evicted by the call (round-4 review F1, Codex P1: a held track decided for a face cut by the tile's edge, with its predicted box past that edge, stayed free, so a smaller face of the same call continued it, an edited person's settings on a newcomer and her own face then someone new at 原圖, or a newcomer evicted it at the cap). So is an away person the call decides for a face it cannot start: claimed for the call, out of every later face's candidates, and taken only by the call that starts the face (F1's away case, recorded 2026-10-23: the same newcomer took an edited person back past the hold, away or past it on that very call, whose face the tile's edge cut).
- **I2. The matching scope never decides identity on its own.** Who a face is comes from the same rules (§7.3 items 2–4, §7.4's gates) over every track, whichever view answers. The call's scope (§6.6) says only what the call does with the answer: feed the pair (the view holds the track's predicted box or the face whole), account for it without feeding (the view's edge cuts both), miss a track (the view holds its predicted box whole and found no face), or start a face.
- **I3. A face's candidate set is the same whichever view answers.** The candidates are every unmatched track and every away person in grace that pass the gates; a track known missing past its hold is one of the away persons by then, weighed as its release makes them, by whichever call answers first (D1), though released only after the call's takes (I4, E3). A face the call leaves takes nobody out of another face's candidates (round-3 review E2: leaving B's face took P, coming back beside B, out of her own face's set, which got a neighbour's settings or 原圖). A call **sees** a track whose predicted box its view can match or miss; a held track is known missing to every call; any other (found elsewhere, or unseen since a no-answer) is **unknown** to it. When the answer turns on an unknown track (it would differ with the unknown tracks left out), the call does not decide: the face is no event for it, and its candidate tracks are neither missed nor evicted by it (they stay every other face's candidates, E2, the unknown track §7.3 item 2 had paired with the face among them: round-6 review Codex #1, left out of the call's candidates once its face was left, it let a smaller face of the call, about as near it as an edited neighbour, be given the neighbour's person and edits); the face is left to the own view of one of those tracks that sees every candidate not held and may start the face (`DetectScope.defers`, `liveViews.ts`). A face whose own decision turns on a candidate of a face the call left (it would differ with that candidate left out) waits for the same answer when it can be left too; else it is decided over all its candidates (E2). That view answers within a cycle. A face left is one event, ended by its answer (round-3 review E1: an entry kept for 4 s from the first leave let the same face's next move, within the grace, be placed at once, so it got a second person at 原圖 or a neighbour's settings): a call that feeds a track with a face at its place, or starts one there, ends it; once that view has answered for the tracks it waits on, the next call that meets the face there places it, and the next call of a view that left it ends it whether or not it meets the face (a face there later is a new event, left again). A call that cannot leave a face, or places it after the answer, cannot tell who it is: a new person at 原圖, never someone the decision over every candidate did not pick (A2; round-3 review E1: round 2 took the unknown tracks to be where they were last found, so a tie between a never-edited B moving into the tile and an edited, pinned A blinking there continued A onto B's face, a swap). No face waits on another view for ever. A layout change and the solo path end every entry.
- **I4. Every person a call decides is taken before any release the call makes.** A release into a full away pool evicts its oldest entry, who may be someone the call decided for another face (C21, D4, E3). By construction: the call's releases (the tracks past their hold, then the tracks evicted for its starts) come after all its takes, and a person decided whose track is past its hold goes on to the new track unreleased. A call evicts only a track it knows to be held and leaves alone: not one whose face, or a face it may own, it paired, accounted for or left (round-3 review E6: a held track whose face the view's edge cut was accounted for, then evicted for a newcomer on the same call, and could not restart while in view at the cap; round-4 review F1: so was one decided by §7.3 item 4 for such a face, I1).

### 7.2 Prediction

Each track keeps a box-centre velocity, an EMA over detections (τ = 200 ms) of Δcentre / Δt. On a detecting frame each track's box is predicted by `velocity × (now − lastSeen)`, at most one box width from where it was. Held tracks are matched at their predicted boxes, so a face that passes behind another and reappears on the far side meets its own track (a swap).

### 7.3 Matching (replaces `faceTracks.ts:103-142`)

1. Dedupe as today (IoU > 0.6, keep the one on a track, else the larger, `:94-102`).
2. Greedy box IoU ≥ 0.3 against predicted boxes, then nearest centre within 0.5 predicted box widths (today's two rules), over every track whichever view answers (I2; §6.6 says what a call does with a pair).
3. **Size gate:** a pair whose box width differs from the predicted box by more than ×1.5 (`TRACK_SIZE_JUMP`) is refused: a face that suddenly doubles is someone stepping in front.
4. **Continuation:** an unmatched detection may continue a **held** track (missing ≤ `LOST_HOLD_MS`) that passes the re-identification gates of §7.4 against its predicted box; this replaces the "one track, one face" rule (`:135-142`), whose job (a lone selfie through a brisk pan) the predicted box and the gates do, without its blind spot (any face anywhere continued a lone track). The held tracks and the away persons (`awayCandidates`) are **one candidate set**, weighed in one decision per detection, largest first, before any eviction or claim (§7.4): a held track wins → continued; an away person wins → claimed when the detection starts its track (item 5); two about as near, whichever kinds → a new person at 原圖. A held track that wins a face the call cannot feed (the view's edge cuts both the face and the track's predicted box) is accounted for, as a pair of item 2 the edge cuts is: reserved for that face, it feeds nobody there and is continued by its own view (I1; round-4 review F1, Codex P1). An away person who wins a face the call cannot start (the view's edge cuts it) is claimed there all the same, so no later face of the call takes them, and is taken by the view that starts the face (I1; F1's away case). (Round-1 review C1 and Codex #1: weighed apart, a person coming back while a neighbour was briefly held continued the neighbour's track and the neighbour then took hers, a swap; and a tie between two held tracks refused here was undone when the full list evicted one of them into the pool just before `claim`.) On the group path the set holds every unmatched track, the ones the call cannot see too (found elsewhere: a track the scope cannot match is still someone in view), and a decision that turns on those is left to a view that sees them (§7.1 I3; round-2 review D5: a track found elsewhere whose face moved a width into a tile that cannot see its place got a second person on that face, who then won it, and its own person was dropped).
5. Unmatched detections start tracks, largest first, each with the person item 4 decided (`take`), taken before any release of the call (I4). At the cap every start of the call is matched with its evicted held track first (a held track the call did not pair, account for or leave, E6), then every decided person is taken, then the tracks past their hold (item 6) and the evicted tracks' persons are released (I4): with the away pool full, a release evicts the pool's oldest entry, who may be a person coming back. (Round-1 review C21: PB's leave-and-return phase, two groups of ten taking turns, brought about a third of the returns back as new persons at 原圖 while the release came first. Round-2 review D4: round 1 ordered it per start, so a larger newcomer, started first, evicted a returner decided for a later face.)
6. A track known missing longer than `LOST_HOLD_MS` (held, or missed by this call) leaves the call's tracks after items 2–3 and before item 4, by whichever call answers: it is not continued, and its person is weighed in item 4 as the away person their release makes them (`awayCandidates`' `pending`: edited or the pinned selection, at the last box, in grace, the pool's cap not yet applied), so they are an away candidate on the very call their face comes back (I3). It is `release`d after item 5's takes (I4); a face decided as its person starts its track with them, unreleased (round-3 review E3: released here, before item 4, into a full away pool, it evicted the pool's oldest entry, a person coming back on that call, so her face got a new person at 原圖; ten such tracks at once lost all ten returners). On the solo path, whose one call sees every track, the tracks dropped are those the build before the review (`8233122`) dropped at the end of the call; their persons are candidates on that call (D1), and nobody the call decides is evicted by their release (E3). A track found elsewhere, or unseen since a no-answer, is not missing and is not dropped (C9). On the group path every call pairs over every track (I2); a pair feeds its track only when the view holds the track's predicted box or the face whole (C5), and a track is missed only by calls whose view holds it (§6.6). (Round-2 review D1: round 1 let only a call that can miss a track drop it, so a far face's track past its hold sat in neither candidate set until its own tile's next call: back on that call, it got an away neighbour's settings, or a new person at 原圖.)

### 7.4 Re-identification (K7)

FaceTracks weighs every candidate of an unmatched face in one decision (§7.3 item 4, I3): the unmatched tracks at their predicted boxes, and the away persons in grace (`LivePersons.awayCandidates`, read-only, the persons of the tracks past their hold included as their release makes them, §7.3 item 6) at their last boxes. A candidate passes when:

- an away person: the time since they were last seen is ≤ `REID_GRACE_MS` = 4000 ms (the 1 s hold included), on the session clock (§7.5); a track: unmatched in this call (one known missing is within the hold, §7.3 item 6);
- the new box centre is within `REID_DIST` = 1.5 × their last box width of their last centre (frame pixels: x scaled by the aspect);
- the new box width is within ×`REID_SIZE` = 1.5 of their last;
- no other candidate passing the gates is within `REID_AMBIGUITY` = 1.25 × the best distance (then nobody is claimed); the candidates are the away persons and the tracks together (§7.3 item 4), so a held neighbour about as near makes a returning face a new person too. On the group path a decision that turns on a track the call cannot see is left to a view that sees it (§7.1 I3).

The nearest such candidate wins: a track is continued, an away person is taken from the pool (`LivePersons.take`) when the face starts its track; no winner, a new person (原圖). (`LivePersons.claim(box, aspect, now)` is the pool alone, `take` of the nearest away candidate, kept for tests; round-2 review D15: this section still named it the decision.) A face entering where a person left, within 4 s and at a similar size, therefore takes their settings: this is the behaviour U5 asked for ("coming back from nearby within a few seconds still counts as the same person"), and the residual risk is §14 R3.

### 7.5 The session clock (K8)

- The away pool ages on live camera time: `pause(now)` when the loop pauses or stops (`live.ts:246-253`, `stop`), `resume(now)` on the next live frame shifts every `lastSeen` by the paused span, as A5 does for a tracker that cannot answer (`faceTracks.ts:81-87`). Review, the editor, 相機已暫停 and a lost engine therefore cost no grace.
- A paused span longer than `LIVE_IDLE_MAX_MS` = 60 s clears the pool and the remembered selection (`resume` does it).
- On loop stop and pause, every track is released into the pool with its last box (today they are dropped, `live.ts:236, 249`), so 重拍 and 恢復相機 re-identify the people in place.
- Null answers keep their A5 behaviour (no aging, no drop). On the group path, where each answer is one view's, a track found before a null stays unseen until a call that can match or miss it answers (§6.6), so a hiccup drops and evicts no track there either; a track held before it stays held, its hold paused (round-3 review E5). A view's no-answer pauses the hold of only the tracks it could miss, each until the next answer (round-5 review G4); a shutter view's is no event (§6.6 Capture).
- **The path (K22).** Each away entry keeps the path that last found them (`release(…, path)`); the loop names its path on every frame (`setPath`). An away person's grace counts only while their path is in use: a 合照模式 toggle stops it (a person dropped after the toggle, last found by the path it left, has spent only their path's time up to the toggle) and turning the mode back runs it again, so the person of a face only one path finds waits out the time on the other path. The other path can still re-identify them as usual, within `REID_GRACE_MS` of their last sighting (a far face that walked closer, say); and nobody is re-identified more than `LIVE_IDLE_MAX_MS` after it. A release with no path named runs the grace as before; `clear` forgets the path.
- A flip, ✕ and a reload end the session (`endCameraSession`): the scene is another one.

### 7.6 Selection and identity

- `selection: 'auto' | LivePersonId`; `target()` = the pinned person if in view (found or held), else the auto person, else null (no face shown).
- The first edit with `selection === 'auto'` pins the auto person (K9).
- A pinned person whose track is dropped: the selection returns to auto and remembers the person (`returnTo`); if they are claimed again before a tap, an edit to someone else or the pool clearing, the selection returns to them. A drag that started on them keeps writing them while they are away (§3.2), and those writes do not cancel the return (round-1 review C11: they did, so the sliders moved to the largest face once the person was back).
- 全部套用's lead is the target at the moment it was turned on; it does not move while on.
- `LiveStats.gpLeftOnLeft` (`live.ts:299-301`) follows the target's track (today the beautified one), so the 左／右 chips stay right for the person being edited.

## 16. 待使用者確認的決定

你說「開始寫」不必停下來問，所以下面這幾件只有你能決定的事，我先選了最接近你說法的做法，照著做下去。不同意的話告訴我，就改。

1. **「所有人的臉都要可以點選」也包含一般距離的合照（K17）**
   - **我的決定：** 不縮小這個要求。手機直立拍時，後鏡頭要找得到約畫面長邊 3% 的臉（大約 3 公尺遠），前鏡頭合照要找得到後排約 5% 的臉（大約 2 公尺），最多 10 人。
   - **為什麼：** 原本的設計只把整個畫面偵測一次。直立拍時，後鏡頭只找得到約長邊 9% 以上的臉，前鏡頭約 12% 以上。三個人肩並肩、後鏡頭 2–3 公尺的一般合照、自拍的後排，大多點不到。
   - **怎麼做：** 拍合照時把畫面分成幾塊放大來找臉（合照偵測），預覽仍保持順暢。人多時臉部效果更新會慢一點，這是你之前已經同意的。
   - **代價：** 工作量比原本多一大塊。預覽裡的臉部效果可能比原本的做法稍微抖一點、跟得慢一點，這些都會量測並設門檻。
   - **達不到門檻時：** 如果桌機上的找臉、流暢度、抖動任何一項沒過，就退回原本只偵測一次的做法，發布說明寫清楚多小的臉找得到，並在這一節記下沒過的門檻和數字。下一步由你決定，例如把偵測移到背景執行緒（另一個專案），或接受這個限制。
   - **你若只需要近距離合照：** 跟我說一聲，就改回只偵測一次的做法，工作量小很多。
   - **桌機量測結果（2026-10-22）：流暢度（PGN）沒過，所以這一版退回只偵測一次的做法（`LIVE_GROUP = false`）。**
